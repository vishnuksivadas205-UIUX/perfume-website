Extrait de Parfum (24% Concentration)

Handcrafted in small maceration batches in Provence. Each flask rests undisturbed for 8 weeks before bottling to achieve maximum roundness and seamless depth.

Alcohol Denat., Parfum (Fragrance), Aqua (Water), Benzyl Salicylate, Linalool, Hydroxycitronellal, Limonene, Geraniol, Citronellol, Coumarin, Farnesol, Eugenol. Formulated strictly according to IFRA standards with zero parabens, phthalates, or artificial colourants. 100% cruelty-free and vegan.

Mist onto pulse points where natural body warmth diffuses the fragrance: inner wrists, base of the neck, and clavicle. Allow the extrait to dry naturally without rubbing, preserving the delicate top notes of pink peppercorn and Italian bergamot.
